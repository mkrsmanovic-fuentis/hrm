## Scoring

The score is the share of correct answers:

```text
Score (%) = (correct answers / total questions) × 100
```

- An attempt **passes** when its score is equal to or greater than the questionnaire's passing score. Otherwise it **fails**.
- Each questionnaire has its own passing score. The default is **80%**.
- A questionnaire counts as completed only after the employee submits it.
- The employee must answer every question before submitting.
- The same attempt cannot be submitted twice.

## Assignment status

Each assignment has one status:

| Status | Meaning |
| - | - |
| Not Started | Assigned, no attempt yet. |
| In Progress | An attempt has been started but not submitted. |
| Passed | An attempt reached the passing score. |
| Failed | The latest attempt was below the passing score. |
| Overdue | The deadline passed and the employee has not passed. |

## Retakes

- Employees can retake a failed questionnaire. There is no limit on attempts.
- Each retake creates a new attempt. Earlier attempts and scores are kept.
- Failed attempts stay available for administrators to review.
- Once an assignment is passed, it cannot be retaken unless an administrator assigns it again.

## Assigning training

- A questionnaire can be assigned to one employee, several employees or a whole department.
- Assigning to a department creates an assignment for every **active** employee in it at that moment. Employees who join later are not included.
- Inactive questionnaires cannot be assigned.

## Access

- Employees can only see their own assignments and results.
- Employees cannot use administrator functions.
- Passwords are stored securely hashed.
- All communication uses HTTPS.

## Data integrity

- Submitted attempts are never changed or deleted.
- Editing a questionnaire does not change earlier attempts or results. Each attempt stays linked to the questionnaire version that was used.
- Deactivating an employee, topic or questionnaire keeps its history.

## Audit log

These administrator actions must be logged, with who did it, what changed and when:

- Creating, editing and deactivating employees, departments, topics and questionnaires.
- Creating assignments.
