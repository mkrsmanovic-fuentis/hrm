Administrators decide who must complete a questionnaire, and by when. You can assign a questionnaire to one employee, several employees or a whole department. The **Assignments** page lists every assignment. Employees cannot open this page.

## Before you start

- You need to be signed in as an administrator.
- You need an active questionnaire in an active topic. See [Manage questionnaires](/user-guide/manage-questionnaires#activate-a-questionnaire).

## Assign a questionnaire

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the form">
    Open **Assignments** in the side navigation and select **Assign training**. A panel opens on the right.

    You can also open a questionnaire's page and select **Assign**. The panel opens with that questionnaire already chosen.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the questionnaire">
    **Questionnaire** offers only active questionnaires in active topics. The topic is shown under each title.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose who gets it">
    Under **Assign to**, choose one of these:

    - **Selected employees**: pick one or more people in **Employees**. Only active employees are offered. Administrators and deactivated people can't be assigned training.
    - **A department**: pick one in **Department**. Only active departments are offered. Everyone who is an active employee in it now gets the training. People who join the department later don't.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the deadline">
    **Deadline** is required. Pick today or a later day. Employees have until the end of that day, in your time zone.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Assign">
    The panel closes, and a message says how many assignments were created. The new assignments appear at the top of the list as **Not started**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## When employees are skipped

An employee who already has open training for the questionnaire doesn't get it a second time. Open training is anything not passed yet: **Not started**, **In progress**, **Failed** or **Overdue**.

After you select **Assign**, a box at the top of the page names each skipped employee and says why. Select **Close** to hide it. If everyone was skipped, the message says that no assignments were created.

An employee who passed the questionnaire earlier can get it again. The new assignment starts as **Not started**, and the earlier one stays in their history.

## See your assignments

The **Assignments** page lists every assignment, newest first, with:

| Column | What it shows |
| - | - |
| **Employee** | The person who must complete the training. |
| **Department** | Their department now. |
| **Questionnaire** | What they must complete. |
| **Deadline** | The last day to complete it. |
| **Status** | Where the employee is. See [Statuses](#statuses). |
| **Completed** | The day the employee passed. **Late** under the date means they passed after the deadline. Empty until passed. |
| **Assigned** | The day the training was assigned. |

- **Search by employee or questionnaire** finds assignments whose employee name or questionnaire title contains the text you type. Upper and lower case do not matter.
- Select **Filters**, then a **Status**, to see only assignments with that status. Select **Clear filters**, or the status **All**, to see them all again.
- Select a column header to sort by it, for example **Deadline** to see the nearest deadlines first. Select it again to reverse the order.

## Statuses

Each assignment has one status:

| Status | Meaning |
| - | - |
| **Not started** | The employee hasn't started the questionnaire yet. |
| **In progress** | The employee started an attempt and hasn't submitted it yet. |
| **Failed** | The employee's latest attempt didn't reach the passing score. They can try again. |
| **Overdue** | The deadline has passed and the employee hasn't passed yet. Overdue is shown in red, so it stands out. |
| **Passed** | The employee passed, even if it was after the deadline. |

Passed comes first: an employee who passes after the deadline is **Passed**, not Overdue, and **Completed** shows **Late**. Employees can still complete overdue training. The full rules are in [Assignment status](/developer-guide/business-rules#assignment-status).

## Change a deadline

You can give an employee more time, or less, as long as they haven't passed yet.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the assignment">
    On the **Assignments** page, find the assignment, for example by searching for the employee or filtering by **Overdue**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Change deadline">
    Select the calendar button at the end of the row. A small window opens with the current deadline. Passed assignments don't have this button.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the new day and select Save">
    Pick today or a later day. The employee has until the end of that day, in your time zone. A message confirms the new deadline.
  </Step>
</Steps>

- If the assignment was **Overdue** and the new deadline is in the future, it is no longer overdue. Its status goes back to **Not started**, **In progress** or **Failed**.
- The employee isn't notified of the change. If they already got a deadline reminder, they don't get another one for the new deadline.
- Each change is recorded with your name and the time.

## What happens next

- Each employee who gets the training sees it in their [notifications](/user-guide/check-your-notifications).
- Each new assignment is recorded with your name and the time.
- Three days before the deadline, employees who haven't passed get a reminder.

## Good to know

- An employee can have several assignments at once, for different questionnaires.
- An inactive questionnaire, or one in an inactive topic, can't be assigned. Training already assigned with it can still be completed.
- The rules are in [Assigning training](/developer-guide/business-rules#assigning-training).
