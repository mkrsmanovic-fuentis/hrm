This page is for employees. The app tells you when a questionnaire is assigned to you, and reminds you when its deadline is close. Notifications appear under the bell at the top of every page.

## Before you start

- Sign in as an employee. Administrators do not get notifications, so they see no bell.

## Read your notifications

<Steps>
  <Step title="Look at the bell">
    The bell is in the top bar, next to your role and name. A number on the bell shows how many notifications you have not read yet. With no unread notifications, the bell shows no number.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the bell">
    The **Notifications** panel opens. It lists your newest notifications first. Unread ones are tinted and have a dot.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a notification">
    The notification is marked as read and your home page opens.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To mark every notification as read at once, select **Mark all as read** at the top of the panel. It appears while you have unread notifications.

## What the notifications say

| Notification | When you get it |
| - | - |
| **New training:** questionnaire title, **due** date | A questionnaire is assigned to you. |
| **Reminder:** questionnaire title **is due** date | The deadline is 3 days away or less. |

Each notification also shows when it arrived.

## Good to know

- You get one reminder per assignment, not one every day.
- If you have already passed the questionnaire, you get no reminder.
- If training is assigned less than 3 days before its deadline, you get both notifications.
- The number on the bell updates when you move to another page, and every minute while the app is open.
- The panel shows your 25 newest notifications. Older ones stay in your account; **Mark all as read** marks them as read too.
- Notifications are in the app only. Email notifications are planned for later.

The rules behind notifications are in [Business rules](/developer-guide/business-rules#notifications).
