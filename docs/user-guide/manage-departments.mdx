Administrators group employees by department. The **Departments** page lists every department with its number of employees, and lets you create, edit, deactivate and reactivate them. Employees cannot open this page.

## Before you start

- You need to be signed in as an administrator.
- Open **Departments** in the side navigation.

## See your departments

The list shows each department's **Name**, **Description**, **Employees** and **Status**. **Employees** counts the active people in the department, administrators included. Deactivated employees are not counted.

- **Search by name** finds departments whose name contains the text you type. Upper and lower case do not matter.
- **Filters** shows only **Active** or only **Inactive** departments. Without a filter, the list shows both.
- Select a column header to sort by it. Select it again to reverse the order.

## Create a department

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select New department">
    The button is at the top right of the page. A panel opens on the right.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the form">
    **Name** is required, up to 100 characters. It must be different from the name of every other department, active or inactive. Upper and lower case do not count as a difference: if **Finance** exists, you cannot add **finance**.

    **Description** is optional, up to 500 characters. Use it to say what the department does.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Create department">
    The panel closes, a message confirms the new department, and it appears in the list as **Active**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Rename or describe a department

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the department">
    Select its row, or the pencil icon at the end of the row.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Change the name or the description">
    The same rules apply as when you create a department. To remove the description, clear the field.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Save changes">
    The panel closes and a message confirms the change. To leave without saving, select **Cancel**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Deactivate a department

Deactivate a department you no longer use. It stays in the list as **Inactive**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select the power icon in the department's row">
    The icon appears only for active departments.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    The dialog explains what happens. Select **Deactivate** to go ahead, or **Cancel** to keep the department active.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Deactivating keeps the department's history, and its employees keep their department. An inactive department can't be chosen for new or edited employees, or for assigning training.

## Reactivate a department

Select the circular arrow icon in the row of an inactive department, then **Reactivate**. The department is active again, with its name, description and employees unchanged.

## Good to know

- Departments are never deleted. Deactivate them instead.
- If the name is already used, the form says so under **Name**. Choose another name.
- Every change you make is recorded with your name and the time, so it is clear who changed what.
