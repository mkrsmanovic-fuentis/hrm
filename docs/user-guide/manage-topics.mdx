Topics are the security subjects your training covers, such as **Phishing Awareness** or **Password Security**. Each questionnaire belongs to exactly one topic, and a topic can have many questionnaires. The **Topics** page lists every topic, and lets you create, edit, deactivate and reactivate them.

## Before you start

- You need to be signed in as an administrator. Employees cannot open this page.
- Open **Topics** in the side navigation.

## See your topics

The list shows each topic's icon and **Name**, its **Description**, **Questionnaires** and **Status**. **Questionnaires** counts every questionnaire in the topic, active and inactive.

- **Search by name** finds topics whose name contains the text you type. Upper and lower case do not matter.
- **Filters** shows only **Active** or only **Inactive** topics. Without a filter, the list shows both.
- Select a column header to sort by it. Select it again to reverse the order.

## Create a topic

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select New topic">
    The button is at the top right of the page. A panel opens on the right.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the form">
    **Name** is required, up to 100 characters. It must be different from the name of every other topic, active or inactive. Upper and lower case, and spaces before or after the name, do not count as a difference: if **Phishing Awareness** exists, you cannot add **phishing awareness**.

    **Description** is required, up to 1,000 characters. Say what employees learn in the topic.

    **Icon** is optional. Pick one from the list of security icons, such as a fish for phishing or a key for passwords. A preview shows the icon you picked. To remove it, select the cross in the field.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Create topic">
    The panel closes, a message confirms the new topic, and it appears in the list as **Active**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Edit a topic

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the topic">
    Select its row, or the pencil icon at the end of the row.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Change the name, the description or the icon">
    The same rules apply as when you create a topic.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Save changes">
    The panel closes and a message confirms the change. If you changed nothing, the panel just closes. To leave without saving, select **Cancel**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Deactivate a topic

Deactivate a topic you no longer use. It stays in the list as **Inactive**, and employees no longer see it. You can't add new questionnaires to an inactive topic or assign its questionnaires, but training that was already assigned can still be completed.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select the power icon in the topic's row">
    The icon appears only for active topics.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    The dialog explains what happens. Select **Deactivate** to go ahead, or **Cancel** to keep the topic active.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Deactivating keeps the topic's history. Past results of its questionnaires stay accessible, and its questionnaires are not changed.

## Reactivate a topic

Select the circular arrow icon in the row of an inactive topic, then **Reactivate**. The topic is active again, and employees see it again. Its name, description, icon and questionnaires are unchanged.

## Good to know

- Topics are never deleted. Deactivate them instead.
- If the name is already used, the form says so under **Name**. Choose another name.
- Every change you make is recorded with your name and the time, so it is clear who changed what.
