## Roles

The platform has two roles.

<Columns cols={2}>
  <Card title="Administrator" icon="user-gear">
    Manages employees and departments, creates topics and questionnaires, assigns training and tracks results.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Employee" icon="user">
    Completes assigned questionnaires, sees results and retakes failed ones.
  </Card>
</Columns>

## Find your way around

After you sign in, your home page opens. The menu on the left lists the pages for your role. Administrators and employees see different menus.

If you open a page that is not for your role, for example from a link someone sent you, the platform shows **No access**. You stay signed in. Select **Go to your home page** to continue.

## How training works

<Steps>
  <Step title="An administrator creates a questionnaire">
    Each questionnaire belongs to a security topic and has multiple-choice questions and a passing score.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The administrator assigns it">
    To one employee, several employees or a whole department, with a deadline.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The employee completes it">
    The employee answers every question and submits. The score is calculated automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The employee passes or retakes">
    A score at or above the passing score completes the training. A lower score can be retaken.
  </Step>
</Steps>
