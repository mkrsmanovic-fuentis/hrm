This page is for administrators and employees. If you forget your password, request a reset link by email. The link lets you choose a new password and signs you in.

## Before you start

- You need access to the email address you sign in with.
- You must have set a password before. If you are new and have not set one yet, use your invite link instead, or ask your administrator to send the invite again. See [Set your password](/user-guide/set-your-password).

## Request a reset link

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select Forgot password?">
    The link is on the **Sign in** page, under the **Sign in** button.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your email">
    Type the email you sign in with in **Email**, then select **Send reset link**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check your email">
    The page says **Check your email**. If the email belongs to an account that can sign in, a reset link is on its way. The page looks the same either way, so nobody can use it to find out who has an account.

    No email? Check your spam folder, or select **Try again** to check the address you typed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Choose a new password

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the reset link">
    The **Reset your password** page opens and shows your email address. The link works for 1 hour.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a password">
    Type your new password in **New password** and again in **Confirm password**. It needs at least 12 characters and cannot be your email address.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Reset password">
    Your new password is saved and you are signed in. Your home page opens with the message **Password changed**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What happens next

- Your old password no longer works.
- You are signed out everywhere else: on other devices and browsers, the **Sign in** page opens the next time you do anything. Sign in there with your new password.

## If the link does not work

| Message | What it means and what to do |
| - | - |
| **This reset link has expired** | The link is older than 1 hour. Select **Request a new link**. |
| **This reset link was used already** | Each link works once. Sign in with your new password, or request a new link. |
| **This reset link is not valid** | You requested a newer link, which replaced this one, or the link was not copied in full. Use the newest link, or request a new one. |

## Good to know

- Only the newest reset link works. Requesting a new one makes the earlier ones stop working.
- Deactivated accounts cannot reset their password. Ask an administrator to reactivate your account.
- In the demo version, no email is sent. After you select **Send reset link**, a **Demo only: reset link** panel shows the link, with **Copy reset link**. It appears only when a link was created.
