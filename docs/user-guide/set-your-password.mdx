This page is for new employees and administrators. When an administrator adds you to the platform, you get an invite link by email. Open it to choose your password. Then you are signed in.

## Before you start

- You need the invite link from your invite email. It works for 7 days.
- If the link has expired, or you cannot find the email, ask your administrator to send the invite again.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the invite link">
    The **Set your password** page opens. It greets you by your first name and shows the email address you will sign in with. You do not need to be signed in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a password">
    Type your new password in **New password**, then the same password again in **Confirm password**. Select the eye icon to show or hide what you typed.

    Your password needs at least 12 characters. A few unrelated words make a strong password that is easy to remember. It cannot be your email address. Spaces count as characters.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Set password">
    Your password is saved and you are signed in. Your home page opens with the message **Password set**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What happens next

From now on, sign in with your email and your new password. See [Sign in and out](/user-guide/sign-in-and-out). If you forget your password, you can [reset it](/user-guide/reset-your-password).

## If the link does not work

| Message | What it means and what to do |
| - | - |
| **This invite link has expired** | The link is older than 7 days. Ask your administrator to send the invite again. |
| **Your password is already set** | You have used this link already. Sign in with your email and password, or select **Forgot password?** to reset it. |
| **This invite link is not valid** | Your administrator sent a newer invite, which replaced this link, or the link was not copied in full. Use the link from your latest invite, or ask your administrator to send the invite again. |

## Good to know

- Each invite link works once. When your administrator sends the invite again, only the newest link works.
- In the demo version, no email is sent. The administrator sees the invite link on the **Employees** page and can copy it for you.
