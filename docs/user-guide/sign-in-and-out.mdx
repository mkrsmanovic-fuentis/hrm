Administrators and employees sign in with their email address and password. You stay signed in for up to 8 hours, even if you reload the page or close the tab.

## Before you start

- You need an account that an administrator created for you, and a password. New accounts set their password through the invite link. See [Set your password](/user-guide/set-your-password).
- Your account must be active. Deactivated accounts cannot sign in.

## Sign in

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the platform">
    If you are not signed in, the platform opens the **Sign in** page. If you followed a link to a page, you return to that page after signing in. If that page is not for your role, your home page opens instead.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your email and password">
    Fill in **Email** and **Password**. Both are required. Select the eye icon in the password field to show or hide what you typed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Sign in">
    The button shows a spinner while the platform checks your details. When they are right, your home page opens. Administrators and employees each have their own home page. The top bar shows your name and your role: **Administrator** or **Employee**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### If you cannot sign in

| Message | What it means |
| - | - |
| **Wrong email or password** | The email or the password is wrong. Check both and try again. The message does not say which one is wrong. If you have not set a password yet, use your invite link. |
| **Account deactivated** | An administrator has deactivated your account. Ask an administrator to reactivate it. |
| **Could not sign in** | Something went wrong on our side. Try again in a moment. |

Forgot your password? Select **Forgot password?** under the **Sign in** button. See [Reset your password](/user-guide/reset-your-password).

## Sign out

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the user menu">
    Select your name in the top bar. The menu shows your name and email.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Sign out">
    You are signed out and the **Sign in** page opens. If the platform is open in other browser tabs, they are signed out too.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Sign out when you use a shared computer.

## Demo accounts

The demo version of the platform has sample data and two demo accounts, so you can see both roles quickly.

- On the **Sign in** page, the **Demo accounts** section has **Sign in as Administrator** and **Sign in as Employee**. Each signs in with one click.
- While signed in, open the user menu and select **Switch to employee** or **Switch to administrator**. You are signed out and signed in again as the other demo account, on that account's home page. Other open tabs switch too.

The demo accounts and the switch are only in the demo version.

## Good to know

- A session lasts 8 hours from the moment you sign in. Working in the platform does not extend it. After 8 hours, the platform asks you to sign in again.
- If your session ends while you are working, the **Sign in** page opens, even if you are only reading a page. Sign in again to return to the page you were on.
- All your browser tabs share one session. Signing in or out in one tab does the same in the others.
- Upper and lower case in your email address do not matter.
